The Phantom of the Opera

Scene I — The Paris Opera House

Status: Draft Date: 2026-10-26 Engine: React Three Fiber Platform: Desktop browser (WebGL 2) Duration: 10–15 min

1. Vision

A first-person, cinematic, music-driven browser 3D experience set in the Paris Opera House (Palais Garnier). The player explores gilded halls, discovers hidden collectibles left by the Phantom, and triggers an escalating atmospheric narrative — culminating in the iconic chandelier drop.

No combat, no puzzles — pure exploration and atmosphere.

Tone: Gothic romance, 19th-century grandeur, creeping dread.

4. Areas

4.1 Grand Foyer Entry Point

A vast gilded hall — marble floors, painted ceiling, ornate columns, mirrored walls. ~30m × 15m × 12m high.

Materials: Procedural marble PBR (white/gray veined, reflective), gilded panels (gold metallic), mirror sections.

Lighting: Baked warm golden ambient. Real-time: 2 chandelier point lights with subtle flicker, wall candle sconces.

Ambient Audio: Footstep echoes on marble, distant ghostly crowd murmur.

Collectibles

①Christine's Letter — Sealed envelope tucked behind a marble bust near the east wall. Proximity: dust motes catch golden light.
④Phantom's Wax Seal — Red skull-stamped seal pressed into a wall panel near the Staircase entrance. Proximity: nearby sconce flickers intensely.

Passage → Grand Staircase: seamless (open archway, no loading).

4.2 Grand Staircase

The iconic double-return marble staircase. Two flights converge at a landing, then split upward. Balustrades, statuary, a high vaulted dome.

Materials: Gold-veined marble variant, ornate railing, painted dome ceiling.

Lighting: Cooler than Foyer (moonlight through high windows). 1 dome light, 2 wall sconces.

Ambient Audio: Wind through high windows, creaking wood, more reverb on footsteps.

Collectibles

②"Angel of Music" Sheet Music — A page wedged between banister rails on the first landing. Proximity: page gently flutters (vertex animation).

Passages → South: seamless back to Foyer. Top: doors to Auditorium (1.5s fade).

4.3 Auditorium

Horseshoe-shaped hall with tiered balconies, ~500 red velvet seats (instanced), proscenium stage with red curtain, and the grand chandelier — the climax prop with the highest detail budget.

Materials: Red velvet seats, gold-trimmed balconies, glass PBR crystals on chandelier, wooden stage floor.

Lighting: Dim, warm — lit only by chandelier (6 point lights) and stage footlights. Box 5 has a cooler, eerie light.

Ambient Audio: Deep silence, occasional seat creak, chandelier crystal tinkling (spatial, from above).

Collectibles

③Phantom's Demand Letter — Pinned to the velvet chair in Box 5. Proximity: Box 5 light flickers cold blue.
⑤"Music of the Night" Full Score — On the conductor's stand in the orchestra pit. Proximity: faint organ note swells.

Passages → Rear doors: fade back to Staircase. Stage-left door: fade to Backstage.

4.4 Backstage Corridor

Narrow, oppressive passage behind the stage. ~3m wide × 20m long × 3m high. Exposed brick, gas lamps, dressing room doors. At the far end: a locked iron door with the Phantom's skull seal.

Materials: Dark red-brown exposed brick PBR, copper pipes, wood doors.

Lighting: Entirely real-time — 4 flickering gas lamps (warm orange). Much darker. Green light seeps from under the locked door.

Ambient Audio: Dripping water, rats, muffled distant organ (low-pass filtered), creaking.

Collectibles

⑥Threatening Note — Slipped under the ajar dressing room door. Proximity: door creaks slightly.
⑦Black-Ribbon Rose — On the floor before the locked door. Proximity: strong phantom presence (shadow, whisper). Final collectible — triggers climax.

5. Player Controls

ActionInputBehavior
MoveWASDCinematic slow walk (~2.5 m/s), no sprint
LookMousePointer lock, subtle camera sway (0.3° amplitude, 2s period)
InteractEPick up / examine when crosshair on interactable within 2m
InventoryTabToggle leather journal overlay
PauseEscapeRelease pointer lock, show pause menu

Player capsule: Rapier kinematic capsule (0.5m radius, 1.8m height). No jump. Eye height ~1.7m.

6. Interaction System

Detection

  • Raycast from camera center, max 3m
  • Within 2m + looking at interactable → prompt [E] Pick up
  • Within 3m → proximity effects only (no prompt)

Proximity Effects

Dust motes (bright particles drifting upward, 1m radius) near uncollected items, plus item-specific cues per area descriptions.

Pickup Flow

  1. Player presses E → controls disabled (0.8s)
  2. Object lifts and rotates toward camera (0.6s ease-out tween)
  3. Crossfade to full-screen parchment overlay (0.2s)
  4. Player reads → click or key to dismiss
  5. Item added to inventory, object removed from world
  6. Music layer threshold check → new layer fades in (4s)
  7. If all 7 collected → climax event

7. Phantom Presence System

Escalating environmental effects. Never a visible character — always indirect.

TierTriggerEffects
Ambient0 itemsBase organ drone. Chandelier crystal tinkle.
Stirring1–2 itemsLights flicker every 30–60s. Distant organ note.
Watching3–4 items+ Spatial whispers behind player (~15s). Rose petals near uncollected items.
Stalking5–6 items+ Dark shadow darts across peripheral vision (0.3s). Gas lamps flicker violently.
Climax7 itemsFull chandelier drop sequence.

8. Climax: The Chandelier Drop

Triggered when the 7th collectible overlay is dismissed. Total duration ~28s.

0.0s — All lights cut to black. Silence.
0.5s — Single organ chord (low C, fortissimo). Sustained.
1.0s — Controls locked. Camera auto-pans toward chandelier.
2.0s — Chandelier sways. Crystal tinkling crescendo.
4.0s — Full orchestral layer erupts: timpani + strings + choir.
5.0s — Chain snaps (spark particles). Chandelier drops.
5.5s — Impact: screen shake, crash sound, crystal shatter, dust cloud.
7.0s — Phantom's laugh echoes (3s, deep reverb).
10.0s — Music subsides to solo melancholic organ. Lights at half.
12.0s — Backstage locked door creaks open. Green light spills out.
18.0s — Camera reaches doorway: spiral staircase descending into darkness.
22.0s — Fade to black.
24.0s — Title card: "To be continued…"

If not in Auditorium: Distant boom + reduced screen shake. Dust falls from ceiling. Text prompt guides player to Backstage. Fallen chandelier visible if player visits Auditorium afterward.

9. Adaptive Music System

Five concurrent Tone.js layers, mixed in real-time.

LayerInstrumentTrigger
1 (base)Organ droneAlways — varies per area
2Strings (sustained)1st collectible found
3Harp arpeggios3 collectibles found
4Choir pad5 collectibles found
5Full orchestral + timpaniClimax event

Per-Area Organ Variations

  • Grand Foyer: Warm, major-key, stately (C major, legato)
  • Grand Staircase: Rising motif (ascending C → G)
  • Auditorium: Mysterious, A minor tremolo
  • Backstage: Dissonant, sparse (tritone intervals, long decay)

Layer activation: volume ramp 0 → 1 over 4s (exponential) + one-shot sting. Area crossfade: 3s linear. Placeholder: Tone.js synthesizers. Production: .ogg loops, 8-bar @ 120 BPM.

10. UI Overlays

HTML overlays on the R3F canvas, styled with Tailwind. Pointer events pass through except when active.

Parchment OverlayFull-screen backdrop (80% black). Aged paper card (600px max, Playfair Display title, Caveat body). Scale-in 0.3s.
Inventory JournalTab toggle. Leather book (CSS texture). Left: item list + count. Right: selected content. RotateY open animation 0.4s.
Pause MenuResume / Restart / Controls on aged-paper card. Triggered by ESC.
Loading CurtainRed (#8B1A1A) full-screen. Gold title. Progress bar. Curtains part on load (CSS clip-path, 2s).
End Card"To be continued…" in gold Playfair on black. "Play Again" button. Golden dust particles.
